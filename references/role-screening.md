# Screen the role before building an application

Verify the live official posting, requisition, deadline, location/work model, compensation, eligibility, portfolio instructions, form questions, and AI-use policy. If unavailable, date the saved source and label the uncertainty. Check prior applications by company plus requisition to avoid duplicates. Do not treat a preferred qualification as a hard requirement.

Before drafting, name the core work, the strongest proof, and the visible form answer most likely to stop the application. Record hidden questions as unverified and revisit fit if a later step reveals a material requirement. Map the decisive requirements to candidate evidence:

| Requirement and priority | Evidence and source | Direct / adjacent / unknown / gap | Where a reviewer can inspect it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Actual duty or condition | Approved claim, artifact, answer, or missing evidence | One status | First-page line, case, form, or note |

**Direct** means demonstrated at relevant scope. **Adjacent** means a similar problem with a different domain or level. **Unknown** needs verification. **Gap** means evidence falls short. State fit and posting status separately. Prioritize direct core matches; pursue a stretch when the candidate understands the material gap. Stop a full build for a closed role or explicit eligibility conflict, and tell the candidate what would change the call.

Record **Apply** when the candidate can prove the core work and meet the known screening conditions. Use **Ask a human** when a specific missing fact or an employer interpretation would change the decision. Use **Pass** for a closed role, confirmed duplicate, or clear mismatch on a required condition.

For each required qualification, point to evidence in the extracted text of the exact file chosen for upload or a confirmed form answer. Include a portfolio case when the employer accepts one as evidence. Move buried evidence up and use accurate role terms. Revisit the fit decision when a real gap remains.
