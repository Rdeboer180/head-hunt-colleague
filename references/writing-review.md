# Tailor without flattening the candidate's voice

Start with the posting, chosen master, evidence ledger, voice guide, and accessible work samples. Choose the headline, first proof, and featured link together. Preserve official chronology and approved claim boundaries. A change of emphasis is normal; a change of fact needs new evidence.

Use the candidate's own positioning voice for the résumé. Put one approved proof point beside the first-page claim, with a direct work sample if available. Use their correspondence voice for the letter and explain one decision or tradeoff the résumé could not fit.

Open the exported résumé link in a fresh browser session when possible. Check that the reviewer can reach the work and quickly see the problem, the candidate's decision, who did what, and what shipped or changed. If the evidence is buried, link to the relevant section if possible or draft a clearer case opening for review. If the evidence is missing, revise the claim or revisit fit.

For a cover letter or short answer, answer the employer's actual question in the first sentence. Connect one real responsibility to a decision the candidate made. Explain the constraint, personal contribution, collaborator roles, and result or observable behavior. Use only motivation the candidate expressed or can substantiate. Mention a material gap when omission would mislead or the form asks. Cut generic praise, interchangeable transitions, tool strings, and résumé recap. A letter is optional when the employer does not request or accept one; keep one for the candidate only if useful.

Use the voice guide as a check, not a caricature. Compare the draft with the candidate's samples or interview wording. Ask whether they would say it aloud. Apply one relevant editorial lens: evidence over assertion, system rule over visual polish, user consequence over activity, or authorship over team blur. Change a sentence only when the lens reveals a real weakness.

Before delivery, check every consequential claim against the evidence ledger. Inspect the exact submitted file, not just the editable source. Confirm PDF text extraction and visual pages, DOCX reading order when supplied, active links, file naming, and employer limits. Save a private note with fit, chosen proof, largest gap, checks run, revisions, and open decisions. If the employer prohibits AI-written material or form assistance, follow the restriction and hand the relevant work to the candidate.
